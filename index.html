Who Is Shreejit?

My name is Shreejit Paudel — a frontend developer and secondary school student from Nepal. I'm driven by a genuine love of building things for the web, one pixel at a time.

My journey started with simple HTML pages and quickly evolved into building multi-page authentication systems, animated login UIs, immersive gaming interfaces, and fully responsive pages. Every project sharpens my skills and pushes my creativity further.

Beyond code, I have a strong interest in graphic design — creating presentations, logos, and visual identities that blend technical execution with real artistic intent.
